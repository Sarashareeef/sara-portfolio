## Crops and transforms to recreate

- **Hero** (`hero/hero-phones-gallery-ribbon.png`, 1536 × 1024). In the design it fills a 1111 × 741 box at 100.05% width, offset −0.1% left and −5.86% top. The browser frame is 682 tall, including its 34.9 bar, and clips the bottom, so roughly 1111 × 647 of the image is visible.
- **User flow** (`diagrams/user-flow-journey.png`, 1947 × 923). It sits in a 1112 × 581 box with a 20px radius. The image is drawn at 131.64% width and 119.56% height, offset −11.02% left and −13.47% top. That crops the sides and top and stretches it slightly, so it does not keep its own proportions.
- **Phone screens** (9 × 1945 × 4096). Each is drawn at about 175 × 368.5 (Pixel 10 Pro mockup). They are in rows of three with 22px gaps, except the first row: 173 / 175 / 173.3 wide, with the middle phone overlapping, at x = 0, 195 and 392.
- **Instagram posts in the problem diagram.** The featured post is rotated 6°, and the background post is rotated −17.11°.
- **Friend avatars.** `friend-avatar-woman.png` is used twice (3:2755, 3:2763), and so is `friend-avatar-man.png` (3:2759, 3:2767).
