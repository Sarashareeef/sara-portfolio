## Notes

- `research/research-patterns.png` (2092 × 752) is shown in Figma in a 1126 × 415 frame at 100% width and 114.11% height, offset -14.11% from the top. That stretches it vertically and crops its top. The file is the uncropped original.
- `doodles/doodle-arrow-a.png` and `doodles/doodle-arrow-b.png` are each reused several times in the Final experience section, at different sizes, rotations and flips. The node lists are in the Use column.
- `doodles/doodle-arrow-b.png` is byte-identical to `public/images/google-maps/doodle-arrow.png`.
- `decor/flow-line-*.png` are Figma's raster exports of the dashed UX-flow connector lines (the source is a line node, not a vector).
- The same dot is used for all four UX-flow connector end dots (one file; Figma exported an identical copy for 3:1520).
- Not exported, as they are shared or code-drawn: `famicons:arrow-back` / `ep:back` (already `public/icons/arrow.svg` and `public/icons/back.svg`), section rules, plain lines and spacer strokes, card borders and fills, and gradient backgrounds such as the Final experience panel gradient.
- Challenge card 03 `arrow-right` (3:1137): Figma returned no image for this node.
- Landing-page images that already exist: `public/images/landing/preview-voyaige.webp` and `folder-voyaige.webp`. They are not used here.
